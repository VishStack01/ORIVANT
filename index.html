05 / Before we talk

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Is this a replacement for my team?

No. It handles repetitive first-response and intake tasks so your team receives better context. Staff remain responsible for sensitive decisions, relationship-building, and requests outside the agreed workflow.

Can you guarantee every answer is correct?

No AI system can honestly guarantee that. We reduce risk through approved knowledge, structured rules, testing, and escalation. Important actions such as booking are confirmed by the connected system, not guessed by a model.

Will it work with our existing software?

Often, where your tools provide suitable APIs or supported integration methods. We verify access, permissions, functionality, and any vendor charges before including an integration in your proposal.

How is our information handled?

Data flows, provider access, retention, and security requirements are agreed during scoping. Using an external AI provider can involve sending selected information to that provider. We do not describe a system as fully local unless it actually is.

Can you support clinics or law firms?

Administrative intake may be possible after reviewing professional obligations, vendor terms, and data-handling requirements. The system is not a substitute for medical advice, legal advice, or a professional conflict check. Sensitive use cases require additional assessment.

How long does implementation take?

A focused implementation may target approximately two to four weeks after required access and approved materials are available. Timing is confirmed in the proposal and can increase for custom integrations or additional reviews.

Can we add WhatsApp or other channels later?

Yes, where supported and appropriately configured. We recommend starting with one reliable website workflow. Additional channels depend on platform approvals, messaging rules, integration access, and separate pricing.
