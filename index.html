A little more clarity

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Not sure how this fits your brokerage? That’s exactly what the discovery call is for.

Is this replacing our team?

No. It handles the first response, approved questions, and structured intake so your team can follow up with context. Sensitive, unsupported, or out-of-scope requests are handed off to a designated person.

Can it work with our current CRM?

Compatibility depends on your CRM’s API, account permissions, and the workflow you need. We check those requirements during discovery and confirm the supported integration in your proposal.

What if it doesn’t know an answer?

The system is designed to use approved business information and hand off when support is missing or conflicting. AI can still make mistakes, so we use testing, restricted actions, backend rules, and ongoing review—not a promise of perfect answers.

What does an implementation include?

The starting scope is one website widget, one business or location, approved FAQs, a defined intake flow, one supported CRM, one calendar, human handoff, and basic reporting. Additional channels or custom workflows are scoped separately.

How is pricing structured?

There’s a one-time implementation fee and an ongoing management fee. Your proposal lists the exact scope, support allowance, and any third-party usage costs before you commit.

How is customer information handled?

Before launch, we agree on the information collected, providers involved, access permissions, retention, and deletion requirements. The workflow should collect only what your team needs. Data may be processed by approved external providers; it is not represented as an entirely local system.

Booking privacy

The company-details form does not save your entries in this page’s local storage or send them to a website backend.

When you continue to scheduling, your entries are included in a Calendly link to prefill the booking form. That link may appear in your browser history, and Calendly processes the information under its own privacy terms.

Completing a booking shares the submitted information with the meeting organizer so they can prepare for and contact you about your appointment. Do not submit confidential client information.
